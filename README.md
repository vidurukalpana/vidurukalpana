## About Me
I'm a software engineer who builds AI systems from start to finish: the **data pipelines** that feed models, the **full-stack applications** people use, and the **evaluation** that proves the system works. I have **3 years of industry experience** building production AI/ML systems that turn large amounts of messy unstructured data into business insight.

- Built **conversation analytics**, **RAG-based search** and **LLM evaluation** systems that run in production
- First-author paper at **IEEE ICEEET 2026**: *A Cost-Effective Intelligent Tutor using Large Language Models*
- Studying for an **MSc in Data Science & AI** at the University of Moratuwa, researching **privacy-preserving conversational AI with Reinforcement Learning**
- **BSc (Hons) in Computer Engineering**, University of Peradeniya
- **Open to long-term roles** in collaborative, product-driven teams (AI, data, full-stack)
- Off the keyboard: national-level badminton player and former captain of the University of Peradeniya women's team 🏸
