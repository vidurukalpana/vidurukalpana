## About Me
I'm a software engineer who builds AI systems from start to finish: the **data pipelines** that feed models, the **full-stack applications** people use, and the **evaluation** that proves the system works.

- Built **conversation analytics**, **RAG-based search** and **LLM evaluation** systems that run in production

## ATM Cash Optimizer: ATM Cash Optimization & Demand Forecasting
_R&D · Individual_
`Python` `PySpark` `LightGBM` `PuLP (MILP)` `DuckDB` `MLflow` `Streamlit`

An end-to-end cash planning system for bank ATMs and CRMs. It combines **quantile demand forecasting** with **mixed-integer optimization** to schedule low-cost refills.
- In backtests on real ATM data, it **cut cash-handling costs by 35–42%** compared with standard bank refill rules.
- Includes a data validation pipeline, scenario simulation, forecast calibration, a backtesting engine, a Streamlit dashboard and CI/CD.

[Repository](https://github.com/vidurukalpana/atm-cash-optimizer) · [Project Page](https://vidurukalpana.github.io/atm-cash-optimizer/)

## CareFlow: Medical Clinic Booking & Queue Management
_Freelance · Individual_
`FastAPI` `PostgreSQL` `React` `TypeScript` `Docker` `AWS CDK` `Bedrock`

A full-stack clinic web app. Patients book online without an account, get SMS reminders and can ask an **AI chat assistant** for help. Staff use a role-based portal for scheduling, QR check-in, walk-ins and live queues.
- I handled everything from requirements and system design to production deployment on AWS (ECS Fargate, RDS, S3, CloudFront, SNS, WAF, Route 53).
- Built with patient-data privacy and security in mind, with automated tests in Pytest.

[Repository](https://github.com/vidurukalpana/medical-clinic-management-web-app) · [Project Page](https://vidurukalpana.github.io/medical-clinic-management-web-app/)
